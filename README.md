# The Uncanny Counter

> "We are the Counters. We hunt evil spirits that escape from the afterlife to protect the living."

## Series Overview

* **Genre:** Supernatural action drama
* **Original Network:** OCN and tvN
* **Total Seasons:** Two
* **Core Premise:** A group of supernatural hunters disguise themselves as employees at a local noodle restaurant while tracking down malevolent spirits that possess human hosts.

## Main Characters

1. **So Mun** 
   The central hero of the story who gains extraordinary physical strength and unique healing abilities.
2. **Ga Mo-tak** 
   A former police detective who brings incredible physical resilience and investigative grit to the team.
3. **Do Ha-na** 
   A sharp investigator who possesses exceptional psychometric tracking skills to locate spirits from miles away.
4. **Choo Mae-ok** 
   The compassionate heart and healer of the group who operates the restaurant front.

## Key Themes Explored

* The pursuit of justice against corruption
* The emotional weight of grief and memory
* The strength found through teamwork and chosen family
